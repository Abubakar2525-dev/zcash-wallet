Zecash Wallet privacy policy

Last updated: November 5, 2026

Important notice. This is an independent third-party application and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or officially connected to the Zecash project or any official Zecash organization.

This policy explains what data the Zecash Wallet app ("the app", "we") collects, how that data is used, shared, protected, kept and deleted, and the choices you have.

1. Data we collect

The app is a non-custodial wallet. It does not require an account, and we do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, location or any other personal information.

We do not run our own servers, and we do not collect, store or have access to any of your data. The app does not include advertising or analytics SDKs, and it does not track you across apps or websites.

2. Data stored on your device

The following data is created and kept only on your phone:

  • Your seed phrase (recovery words), private keys and viewing keys
  • Your wallet addresses, balances and transaction history
  • App settings, such as your chosen lightwalletd server and biometric lock preference

The app never uploads your recovery words, viewing keys or private keys to any server, dashboard or panel.

3. How data is used

Data on your device is used only to run the wallet: to show your balance and history, build and sign transactions, and display prices. We do not use any data for advertising, profiling or marketing, and we do not sell user data.

4. Data shared with third-party services

To work, the app connects directly from your device to the services below. Only the data needed for each request is sent. As with any internet connection, these services can see your device's IP address.

  • CoinGecko: requests for the ZEC price chart. No wallet data or personal data is sent.
  • A public Zecash block explorer: your own transparent addresses, to list your transparent coins and balance.
  • NEAR Intents (1click.chaindefuser.com): only when you request a Private Send or CrossPay quote, the assets, amount, destination address and refund address for that swap.
  • The lightwalletd server you choose: requests to sync the blockchain and broadcast transactions you have signed. Shielded data is decrypted on your device, not on the server.

Each of these services handles data under its own privacy policy. Transactions you send are recorded on the public Zecash blockchain, as is the case for any Zecash wallet.

5. How we keep data secure

  • Your seed phrase and keys are encrypted on the device using the Android Keystore and never leave your phone.
  • All network traffic to the services listed above is sent over encrypted HTTPS/TLS connections.
  • Transactions are signed locally on your device; private keys are never transmitted.
  • You can turn on an optional biometric or device lock to stop others from opening the wallet.
  • Because we run no servers and hold no user data, there is no central database of user information that could be breached.

6. Camera and biometrics

The camera is used only to scan a payment QR code. Images are processed on your device in real time and are not saved or sent anywhere.

Biometric unlock is optional and uses Android's built-in biometric system. Your fingerprint or face data is handled by Android and is never accessed or stored by the app.

7. Data retention and deletion

Wallet data stays on your device until you delete it. You can delete all app data at any time by removing the wallet in the app, clearing the app's storage in Android settings, or uninstalling the app. We cannot recover a deleted wallet, so make sure your seed phrase is backed up safely before deleting.

Since we do not keep any of your data, there is nothing for us to delete on our side. Data received by third-party services is kept under their own policies.

8. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.

10. Contact us

For privacy questions or requests, please contact us through the developer contact details on the app's Google Play Store listing.
