Zcash privacy

Last updated: November 2, 2026

Important notice. This is an independent third-party application and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or officially connected to the Zcash project or any official Zcash organization.

Your wallet data

Zcash is a wallet for the Zcash network. The seed phrase, viewing key, and private key are created and stored only on this phone, encrypted with the Android Keystore.

The app does not upload recovery words, viewing keys, or private keys to a server, dashboard, or panel.

Services the app contacts

  • CoinGecko, for the ZEC price chart
  • A public Zcash explorer, to list your own transparent coins
  • NEAR Intents (1click.chaindefuser.com), when you request a Private Send or CrossPay quote
  • The lightwalletd server you choose

Camera and biometrics

The camera is used only to read a payment QR. Biometric unlock is optional.

Contact us

For privacy questions, please contact us through the Play Store listing.
